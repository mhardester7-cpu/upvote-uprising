# UPVOTE UPRISING Community Requests and Discovery Guide

This is the running list of additions people asked for during the social-media
community series. It is grouped by the day assigned to the request, and the
entries within each day follow their actual implementation order.

Current through **Day 7 — September 1, 2026**.

Routine bug fixes, balance changes, rendering polish, animation cleanup,
security work, and other normal gameplay improvements are deliberately omitted.

> **Spoiler warning:** This guide explains where the requested additions are
> and exactly how to trigger them.

## Before you start

- Choose **SOLO SURVIVAL** for the simplest way to see the complete set.
- Use `E` to interact. On touch devices, use **USE** instead.
- The facility floor plan changes with the world seed, so compass directions
  are not fixed. Named rooms and their progression order remain recognizable.

## Day 1 — August 25, 2026

### 1. The important red button

**What was added:** An extremely serious-looking wall button that deliberately
has no useful effect.

**How to find it:** Start a survival run and inspect the walls from waist to eye
level in **THE ROOM YOU WOKE IN**. Stand close and aim directly at the
illuminated red button until **PRIORITY OVERRIDE** appears, then press `E`.

## Day 2 — August 26, 2026

### 1. Interplanetary travel

**What was added:** The Starling spacecraft, launch and flight controls,
planetary approaches, and playable landings on volcanic **Cinder Reach** and
icy **Nyx Glacier**.

**How to find it:** In **THE ROOM YOU WOKE IN**, approach the exterior shutter
until the HUD reads **OPEN SPACEPORT AIRLOCK — FREE** and press `E`. Walk to
the Starling outside and press `E` to board.

**How to use it:** Press `W` to launch. Use `W`/`S` for thrust and braking,
`A`/`D` or the mouse to steer, and `Shift` to boost. When the ship stops at
a planet and displays the landing prompt, press `Space`. After touchdown,
normal on-foot survival continues on that planet.

### 2. A human voice for the important button

**What was added:** The useless red button gained a recorded human vocal
reaction.

**How to hear it:** Find the Day 1 button in **THE ROOM YOU WOKE IN**, aim at it
until **PRIORITY OVERRIDE** appears, and press `E`.

## Day 3 — August 27, 2026

### 1. Dinosaur Factory

**What was added:** A velociraptor-cloning machine that creates an animated
companion which follows the player, hunts zombies, and bites them.

**How to find it:** Check the interior doors leading from the starting room and
use their HUD prompts to find **DINOSAUR FACTORY**. Look for the large machine
with a glass incubation pod and green control panel.

**How to use it:** Save 1,500 coins, stand at the control side, and press `E`.
After roughly four seconds, a velociraptor hatches. One factory raptor can be
deployed per player at a time.

### 2. Chicken-nugget dispenser

**What was added:** A healing machine that serves six physical chicken nuggets.

**How to find it:** The dispenser is always inside **THE ROOM YOU WOKE IN**.
Stand beside it and press `E` when the HUD reads **6 CHICKEN NUGGETS · +25 HP**.
It heals 25 HP and takes 30 seconds to restock.

### 3. the game project social links

**What was added:** Front-menu links for the game project on Instagram, YouTube, TikTok,
and Reddit.

**How to find them:** Open the game and look beneath the UPVOTE UPRISING title for the
**FOLLOW the game project** banner.

### 4. Global play counter

**What was added:** A persistent all-time count of game sessions.

**How to find it:** Wait for the server-status row at the bottom of the main
menu. When the service is available, it displays current players and
**TOTAL PLAYS**.

## Day 4 — August 28, 2026

### 1. Human Observation

**What was added:** A reverse aquarium where fish move through a water conduit
above a furnished human enclosure.

**How to find it:** Continue beyond the Dinosaur Factory into a deeper indoor
room. Look for the cyan shutter and **HUMAN OBSERVATION** sign. Stand by the
shutter and press `E` to open it. Walk beneath the central viewing chamber and
wait for the fish to stop and observe the people below.

**How to use the refuge:** Press `E` at the cyan shutter again to close it
behind you and temporarily keep zombies outside.

### 2. Release 0.0.0 changes card

**What was added:** A compact satirical release-notes card beside the main menu.

**How to find it:** Load the website and wait for the initial main menu. The
**RELEASE 0.0.0** card appears beside that first menu and can be dismissed with
its close button or `Escape`. It does not return on the pause screen or after a
run has started.

### 3. John Pork planet

**What was added:** Cinder Reach became the John Pork planet, with his image
covering the complete spherical surface.

**How to find it:** Open the spaceport, board the Starling, and launch. Follow
the first-flight guidance toward **CINDER REACH**, or steer toward the orange
planet manually. John Pork is easiest to see from space during the approach.
Press `Space` at the landing prompt to visit the surface.

## Day 5 — August 29, 2026

### 1. Free facility doors

**What was added:** Every facility progression door became free while remaining
physical and interactive.

**How to use it:** Approach any closed progression door and press `E`. This
includes normal room doors, the **SPACEPORT AIRLOCK**, and the reclosable
**HUMAN OBSERVATION** shutter. The Dinosaur Factory still costs 1,500 coins
because that price buys the raptor rather than opening a door.

## Day 6 — August 30, 2026

### 1. Rhythm Tower and gorilla drummer

**What was added:** A 24-metre climbable industrial tower with a realistic
gorilla drummer, full drum kit, animated performance, spatial music, physical
rooftop deck, and working ladder.

**How to find it:** Explore toward the middle of the generated facility and look
for the open-air room labeled **RHYTHM TOWER**. Walk around the base until the
HUD says **USE TOWER LADDER**, then press `E`.

**How to use it:** Use `W` and `S` to climb or descend and `Space` to jump
off. Climb through the deck hatch to reach the gorilla's performance. The music
is heard as a rooftop performance rather than everywhere in the facility.

### 2. Inevitable snail and banana gun

**What was added:** A realistic inevitable snail that pursues the player on
foot, glowing banana asteroids, the banana-craft transformation, and a stored
banana charge that becomes a 30-second banana-boomerang gun after landing. The
spacecraft now has a framed canopy, swept wings, twin engine pods, and a tail
fin around its intact-banana hull. On foot, the unpeeled banana is mounted to a
proper grip, trigger, guard, front sight, and muzzle instead of being carried like
ordinary fruit.

**How to find the snail:** It begins at least 14 metres away and, whenever a
clear exterior point exists, outside the room where the player wakes. Watch the
floor beyond the opening room rather than searching spawn itself. To avoid a
permanent banner, the **INEVITABLE SNAIL** distance warning appears only when it
gets within 3 metres. It crawls head-first along the ground, routes around solid
props, follows whenever the player is on foot, disappears while the player is
aboard the Starling, and causes instant death on contact.

**How to unlock the banana gun:**

1. Board the Starling and launch into space.
2. Fly through a large glowing banana asteroid. Several sit near the assisted
   routes to Cinder Reach and Nyx Glacier.
3. Confirm that the HUD says **BANANA CRAFT — CHARGED**.
4. Continue to a planet and press `Space` at its landing prompt.
5. Once back on foot, the 30-second banana-gun timer begins automatically. Aim
   and hold Mouse 1 to throw boomerangs. Hitting the snail knocks it roughly 12
   metres backward and briefly slows it. A boomerang can also hit a zombie, but
   deals only 2 damage, so it remains a novelty and snail-control tool rather
   than replacing a real weapon.

### 3. Gaze-triggered spinning cat

**What was added:** A flat spinning-cat GIF that transforms into a realistic
animated 3D tabby when watched, then wanders around its room.

**How to find it:** Explore the ordinary indoor rooms beyond the named community
landmarks and scan the floor for the realistic tabby. Its room changes with the
world seed, but it never shares **THE ROOM YOU WOKE IN**, **DINOSAUR FACTORY**,
**HUMAN OBSERVATION**, or **RHYTHM TOWER**. Look directly at it from within
roughly 13 metres; no button press is required. Keep watching while the 2D GIF
spins and transforms into the 3D cat, then wait for it to begin walking around
the room.

## Day 7 — September 1, 2026

### 1. Clippy wave-five mini-boss

**What was added:** Exactly one 650-health Clippy joins wave five as an
unarmoured mini-boss. Its animated paperclip silhouette, eyes, pupils, hopping
movement, boss health bar, announcement, and adaptive-music pressure are unique
to the encounter.

**How to find it:** Start **SOLO SURVIVAL** or a co-op horde and reach wave
five. Clippy leads the spawn queue for that wave, so the health bar and
**CLIPPY HAS A SUGGESTION** feed message appear while the horde is still active.

### 2. Explosive banana vending machine

**What was added:** A physical banana vending machine beside the gorilla on the
Rhythm Tower roof. Shaking it starts a one-shot 1.45-second rattle, destroys the
machine, and launches 320 bananas into the weather. The trigger and destroyed
state are shared authoritatively in co-op, so every player sees the same event.

**How to use it:** Climb the Rhythm Tower ladder, approach the yellow machine on
the rooftop deck, and press `E` at **SHAKE BANANA MACHINE**. On touch devices,
tap **USE**. The machine cannot be triggered a second time during that run.
