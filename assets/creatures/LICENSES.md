# Creature assets

- `spinning-cat.gif` — user-supplied transformation reference.
  Source: https://i.redd.it/fz5j7m4dvbmh1.gif
  The source page did not provide author or licence metadata; confirm
  redistribution rights before a public release.

- `cat/maggie-animated-v3.glb` — “Cat named Maggie” by ChamberSu1996,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0. The unused static presentation duplicate was
  removed; textures were converted to core PNG and capped at 512px for broad
  browser/mobile support. The realistic skinned quadruped and authored walk
  cycle are preserved.
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/7a078f6d97c748e3b19a042d71e2f012

- `inevitable_snail/snail-v2.glb` — “Snail in the Water” by Rafael Rodrigues,
  licensed under CC BY 4.0. The presentation leaf, water plane, and mirrored
  duplicate were removed; the complete animal and authored embedded PBR
  materials are preserved with a mobile-safe 512px maximum.
  Source: https://sketchfab.com/3d-models/28f1adcb72c140e29300d1dfe9cff8fd

- `gorilla/gorilla-rigged.glb` — “Gorilla (male)” by Micket, dedicated to the
  public domain under CC0 1.0. Original Blender geometry converted to GLB 2.0;
  a deterministic seven-bone armature and skin weights were added for hand-held,
  beat-synchronised drumming, and the model is re-materialled at runtime.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/gorilla-male

- `gorilla/gorilla-fur-cc0.jpg` — “Gray cat fur texture” by PPD,
  dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0. Used as the skinned gorilla's
  albedo and subtle bump detail.
  Source: https://pixnio.com/textures-and-patterns/fur/gray-cat-fur-texture

- `velociraptor/Velociraptor.fbx` — “Animated Dinosaur Pack” by Quaternius,
  dedicated to the public domain under CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://quaternius.com/packs/animateddinosaurs.html
- `dinosaur_skin/lizard_skin.png` — “Reptile Skin” by RooMan93, dedicated to
  the public domain under CC0 1.0.
  Source: https://opengameart.org/content/reptile-skin

The generated attribution index is also available in `assets/CREDITS.json` and
the project-root `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.
